Money you received never shows on the day you got it

Each received payment is now a real row (a Salary payday, a gift from mom), so the calendar can show it. This page proposes how a day shows income without making it look like spending, how you fix one payment that came late or short, and how a payday that has not come yet looks.

  4. No way to fix one payment MediumA salary paid 2 days late or 1tr short can only be changed by editing the schedule, which changes every month. PUT /transactions/:id exists since PR 1; no screen uses it.
  5. A coming payday is invisible on the calendar Medium25 Nov looks like any other day, so "can I pay rent on the 28th?" needs the Income page. Planned spendings already show as ~ estimates, income does not.

02Who and when

Salaried parent

As someone paid on the 25th, I want to see payday on the calendar so that I know which bills land before and after it.

Family member

As a daughter who got 500k from mom, I want it on the day it came so that the day's story is complete.

Freelancer

As someone paid late and short, I want to fix that one payment so that the month is true without touching my schedule.

Careful spender

As someone watching the month, I want received and expected income apart so that I never spend money that has not arrived.

03Directions

All three use the same data and the same edit sheet. They differ in how loud income is in the month grid and how the day list is split.

A

Green second line

Recommended

The spend total keeps its place. Income gets its own small green line under it: solid when received, faint with ~ when expected. The day list is one time-ordered list where money in is green and signed +.

Solves 123456
Good
  • Same pattern as planned spendings (~, muted), so nothing new to learn.
  • Daily Summary becomes "Out / In" for the day; the month's income moves to a small line.
  • Heat fill stays spend-only.
Watch
  • Cells on phones fit one line: show income only when the day has no spend, or as a green dot.
  • Two numbers in one cell on a busy day.
B

Payday dot

The grid stays almost as it is: a green dot by the date says "money came in" (a ring when it is still expected). Amounts show only in the day panel, which splits into "Money in" above "Money out".

Solves 123456
Good
  • Calmest grid; works the same at phone width.
  • Two sections make income impossible to mistake for spending.
Watch
  • How much came in needs a tap.
  • Two lists split one day's story; time order is lost.
C

Day net

Each cell shows the day's net: +380k in green when more came in than went out, the spend total otherwise. The day panel opens with In, Out and Net, then one list.

Solves 1234
Good
  • One number per cell, no extra row.
  • Answers "did I end the day ahead?".
Watch
  • Payday hides that day's spending (20.000k − 85k reads as +19.915k).
  • Spend totals and nets mixed in one grid are hard to compare; expected paydays have no place.

04Recommendation

Build A. It shows the amount where you already look, reuses the muted ~ style for "not yet", and keeps green for money in only. On phones the cell falls back to B's green dot when the day also has spending. The edit sheet is shared by every direction.
