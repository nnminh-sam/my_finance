The numbers are right, but the app shouts, hides the answer, and belongs to one person

This page takes today's calendar as the base and adds the research plan on top, one layer per week: Calm (look), Clear answer (safe to spend, coming up, pace), Trust (export, import, privacy) and Family (who paid). Nothing moves money, nothing is locked, no bank sync.

02Who and when

Paid on the 5th

As someone 4 days into a pay cycle, I want one number for today so that I know if the coffee is fine without doing maths.

Bill payer

As the one who pays điện, mạng and Spotify, I want the next 7 days in one list so that no due date surprises me.

Couple, one book

As two people sharing costs, I want “paid by” on every row so that month end has no “ai chi bao nhiêu?”.

Leaving Money Lover

As someone with 4 years of records, I want them in and out in one tap so that I never feel locked in again.

On the bus

As someone logging in public, I want amounts hidden so that the person next to me reads nothing.

03Directions

Not four alternatives: four layers that stack, in build order. The stage below is the whole app with every layer on. Step back to Today to compare. A–D then zoom into what each layer adds.

A · week 1 · ~5 h

Calm

Recommended first

No new feature, no layout change. Real spending stays loud, forecasts go grey, the blue fill becomes a 2px bar, and colour is kept for three meanings: money left, money in, over. Pick the palette here; every stage on this page follows.

Solves 1238910
Good
  • CSS and tokens only: no data, endpoint or layout change.
  • Committed plans (is-sure) read one step darker than estimates, so “sure” finally shows in the grid.
  • Today is a filled date badge, not a glowing box.
Watch
  • 143 uses of --primary: add the new tokens as aliases first (no visual change), then change values one by one.
  • Ink + jade uses one hue for “left” and “income”. Fine in the sidebar, check Analytics charts.
  • Warm white is a light theme; the app's dark default would need a second tuned set.
B · week 2 · ~2 days

Clear answer

The sidebar leads with Safe to spend today, the value the app already computes. Under it: what is due in the next 7 days with a reminder bell, and the pace sentence drawn as a chart that ends on “how much will be left”. Three states of the same panel:

Solves 45
Good
  • No new data: perDay, daysLeft and plan occurrences already exist.
  • Over income becomes an action (“Trim plans”), not a red number.
  • On phones the hero and Coming up sit above the month grid (switch the main stage to Phone).
Watch
  • The sidebar gets longer; the mini calendar moves below Coming up or behind a toggle.
  • A reminder needs a channel. In-app only is free; push or email is new infrastructure.
  • “Safe today” is left ÷ days, so it jumps after a big bill. The sub-line keeps the total visible.

04Recommendation

Build the layers in order A → B → C → D; each one ships alone. Start with A in Ink + jade: it is about 5 hours of CSS, it cuts the screen to two meaning colours plus grey, and it stops jade/green fighting a blue brand. Keep Warm white as the future light theme. Three answers decide the rest: the palette, whether private rows are hidden or counted, and whether reminders stay in-app.
