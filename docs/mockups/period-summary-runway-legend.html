Runway bar: legend options

Direction A with the four equal legend cards replaced. The cards wasted space because each held a short number in a quarter of the width. They also repeated “Left”, which is already the headline. Every option below drops the repeated Left amount and ties the blue swatch to the headline instead.

“This cycle” uses your real numbers. The other buttons are made-up edge cases. Hover any amount to highlight its part of the bar.

A1

Labels under their segment

Each amount sits directly under the part of the bar it describes, so the legend is the bar’s own caption.

  • WhyNo swatches to match up. Position tells you which amount is which, and the empty space disappears.
  • Trade-offSmall segments push their labels sideways. When labels can’t fit in one row, they stack into a list.
  • TrySwitch to Phone width to see the stacked fallback.
A2

One-line legend

Swatch, name, amount and percentage in a single row, with income pinned to the right.

  • WhyThe simplest change: about 40px of height instead of 90, and every item is only as wide as its text.
  • Trade-offAmounts are smaller than the card version, so the headline carries the weight.
  • TryPhone width turns the row into a right-aligned list.
A3

Breakdown beside the hero

The headline and bar on the left, a compact table on the right that fills the space the headline leaves empty.

  • WhyUses the wide card’s right side. The table adds up to a total (Going out), which the cards never showed.
  • Trade-offThe bar is shorter, so thin segments get thinner.
  • TryHover a row. Below 760px the table moves under the bar.
A4

Amounts in the bar

A taller bar with each amount printed inside its segment. Segments too narrow for text get a label underneath.

  • WhyThe most compact: no legend row at all when every segment is wide enough.
  • Trade-offText inside coloured fills needs care for contrast. Amounts shorten to “8,2M” when space is tight.
  • TryPhone width: amounts shorten, then move under the bar.
A5

Plain sentence

The breakdown as one sentence under the bar, with swatches inline.

  • WhyReads naturally and leaves zero values out (“nothing committed yet”) instead of showing a greyed 0 ₫.
  • Trade-offHarder to scan for a single number, and the wording has to cover every case.
  • TrySwitch scenarios to see how the wording changes.
