MyFinance · Quick entry for web and phone

Three directions for one keypad-first input

The phone's keypad dock (amount → category → ✓) becomes the input everywhere, and it grows to cover everything the current Add Transaction dialog does: description, date, time, repeat rules, the “Add more” queue, editing, and income. Each direction is shown twice, on the new phone UI (direction F) and on the current desktop web app. All of them are clickable.

Try it: type an amount (the web frames accept the keyboard too), pick a category, open the details, press + Next to queue, then save. Switch to Income in each one. Nothing is saved anywhere.

AKeypad + detail chips

The dock you have now, plus a row of detail chips under the amount (Date · Time · Note · Repeat). A chip swaps the keypad for its panel; Done brings the keypad back. The free key next to 0 becomes + Next, which queues the entry and clears the amount. On the web the same pieces sit side by side in one dialog: keypad on the left, every field on the right, the queue at the bottom.

Phone · Week tab
Web · current desktop, + in the header

Strengths

  • Smallest change from what you just shipped; the 3-tap path is untouched.
  • Every field is one tap away and shows its value on the chip.
  • Web dialog shows everything at once, good for batch entry.

Weaknesses

  • The dock gets taller (chips row + tray) and covers more of the day's list.
  • Repeat options are dense inside a phone panel.
  • The web dialog is wide; it needs a fallback layout under ~820 px.

BStep sheet

Entry as three short steps: Amount → Category → Details. Picking a category moves on by itself, so a plain expense is still amount, tap, ✓. Details is a grouped list (description, date, time, repeat) ending in Add another or Save. On the web the steps become a left rail that shows each answer, like a checkout, plus a Review step for the queue.

Phone · sheet over Week
Web · stepper dialog

Strengths

  • Each screen does one thing; large targets, easiest to make accessible.
  • Room for big category tiles, even with 13+ categories.
  • Review step makes the queue explicit before saving.

Weaknesses

  • A sheet over Week hides the day's list while you enter.
  • Changing one field means stepping back.
  • On the web, steps feel slower than a single form for power users.

CSentence + receipt

The entry reads as a sentence: “Spent 45.000 ₫ on Breakfast & Lunch for Breakfast, today at 08:30, once.” Every bold part is a token; tap one and the area below becomes its picker (keypad for the amount). Queued entries stack above as receipt lines with a running total. On the web the sentence is large and keyboard-first (Tab moves between tokens) with the receipt in a side column.

Phone · sheet over Week
Web · composer dialog

Strengths

  • The whole entry is readable at a glance before saving.
  • Repeat and income read naturally (“every month from 10 Nov”).
  • Receipt makes batch entry feel like one shopping trip.

Weaknesses

  • Most novel pattern; needs a short first-run hint.
  • Sentence grammar must be translated carefully if the app is localised.
  • Tokens wrap unpredictably at large text sizes.
