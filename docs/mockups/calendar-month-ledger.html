02Who and when

Parent who logs

As the one who logs most spending, I want to see every item on a busy day so that I can catch a missing or doubled entry.

Partner on a phone

As a partner checking on my phone, I want to open a day without losing the month so that I can compare it with the days around it.

Payday planner

As the person who plans the pay cycle, I want upcoming bills and expected income on their days so that I know what is safe to spend before the 5th.

Looking something up

As a family member, I want to find "when did we pay electricity?" so that I can answer without scrolling day by day.

03Directions

Each stage works: click a day (A also filters by tile). B and C keep a Desktop / Phone switch. Same data in all three.

A

Day panel · desktop

Recommended

An edge-to-edge grid where each day shows one number: its net (received − spent, plus what is still planned). The sidebar keeps the period summary on top; below it the selected day shows its net, tiles for Spent / Received / Planned that also filter the list, a single + button, and a plain label + amount list that scrolls. Phone gets its own design later.

Solves 1345
Good
  • One number per day: sign + colour say in or out, amber says over budget, grey says planned
  • "₫" is stated once (grid corner, sidebar), so the full amount fits; narrow cells switch to 1,5tr
  • Tiles answer "where did it go" and filter the list in one click
Watch
  • Net hides a big spend on a payday (5 Nov reads +24.857.000)
  • When a day has one kind only, the tile repeats the net
  • Phone (issue 2) is out of scope here
B

Month + ledger

A small month grid with a spend bar per day sits beside a scrolling ledger of the whole month, grouped by day. Clicking a date jumps the ledger; category chips filter it.

Solves 12345
Good
  • Best for finding a payment (use case 4)
  • One scroll reviews the whole month on a phone
Watch
  • Cells lose their amounts; the grid becomes a navigator
  • Overlaps with the Transactions ledger page
  • Loads the full month of rows at once
C

Week opens

All weeks stay short (date + total). Clicking a day or week number opens that week: its cells grow and list every transaction with a category stripe. A week total sits at the end of each row.

Solves 1345
Good
  • Shows a whole week of detail side by side
  • Week totals answer "how was this week?"
Watch
  • Rows change height, so the month swipe pages need variable heights
  • On a phone the open week turns into a list under the row, close to A's sheet

04Recommendation

A · Day panel (desktop). It keeps the calendar people know, moves every detail into one sidebar, and leaves each day with a single signed number. The phone calendar gets its own design next.
