Stroke width is 2 everywhere except DayPanel.tsx:57 (Plus at 2.4), the one emphasis exception. Lucide adds aria-hidden="true" when no label is passed, so decorative icons need no extra prop. Icon-only buttons still need an aria-label.

Known app inconsistencies (not fixed in src/ yet):
  • F-01 Period chevrons render at 14, 16, 18, 20 and 22px. Target: 20 for page and period nav, 16 for inline steppers.
  • F-02 Close X renders at 15, 16, 18 and 20px. Target: 20 or 18 for dialogs, 16 for inline cancel and remove.
  • F-03 Same-meaning icons drift: Sun/Moon at 15, 18, 20; Plus at 16, 20, 24; RefreshCw at 16, 20; Trash2 at 15, 16; Pencil, Search, Check, SkipForward and ChevronDown at 13 to 18.
  • F-05 Income-source icons differ. IncomeSources uses Laptop, Wallet and TrendingUp; quick entry uses Coffee, Heart and CircleDollarSign for the same sources.
  • F-06 The calendar page uses LayoutGrid on desktop and CalendarDays on mobile. CalendarRange means Plans in the nav, planned spend in DayDetails, and pay period in budget headings.
  • F-07 The category icon map is copied into five components (WeekView, DayDetails, CategoryManager, PlannedSpendings, mobile/MobileUi) with default sizes 12 and 16. There is no shared CategoryIcon yet.
  • F-09 The comment in AuthScreen.tsx says "deep brand blue", but the brand is jade #2f7566.
